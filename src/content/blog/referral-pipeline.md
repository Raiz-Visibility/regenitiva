## Where Referrals Actually Come From

Most practices assume regenerative medicine referrals will come from marketing. In year one, the majority come from inside the practice — existing patients who are already being seen for pain and injury.

## The Internal Referral Path

The physicians and staff who see patients every day are the ones best positioned to identify candidates. The conversation isn't a pitch — it's a clinical discussion about available options.

- Identify patients who have exhausted conservative care but aren't surgical candidates
- Present regenerative medicine as one option among several, not the default
- Document the discussion and the patient's decision in the chart

## Building External Referral Relationships

Once the internal pipeline is running, external referrals follow. Other physicians in the area need to know what you offer and, more importantly, that you have the documentation and compliance structure to back it up.

## Takeaways

Start with the patients already in your waiting room. The external pipeline builds on the credibility of outcomes you can document.
