## The Difference Between a Sign-Off and Training

Most practices have a sign-off sheet. Fewer have a training program that produces staff who can reliably execute a protocol and document what they did. The gap between the two is where audit findings live.

## What Real Competency Training Covers

Competency training should cover the full scope of what each staff member is responsible for — not just the procedure, but the documentation, the setup, and the post-procedure workflow.

- Preparation and handling of materials
- Procedure-specific setup and positioning
- Documentation requirements during and after the procedure
- Recognition of complications and the response protocol

## How to Document It

Each competency check should produce a dated record that names the specific skills verified, who verified them, and when. A blanket signature on a generic form doesn't meet the standard.

## Takeaways

Training is an ongoing program, not a one-time event. The documentation should show what was taught, when it was verified, and that verification happens on a schedule.
