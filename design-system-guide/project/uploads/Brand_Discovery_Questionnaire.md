**#1 reason customers come to you**
Our customers are doctors. To grow revenue by adding a profitable regenerative medicine service line without having to build everything themselves.

**Ideal customer**
Physicians — particularly physiatrists, sports medicine doctors, and pain doctors.

**Problem you solve for them**
They are able to perform PRP injections (spine, cervical, and joint) in an office-based setting, allowing for better return on investment.

**Best-fit customer profile**
