Regenerative Medicine Practice Development

A complete, compliant program built around your existing practice — not a kit and a handshake.

This service covers everything required to add regenerative medicine to your practice: protocol design, staff training, compliance documentation, and the operational structure to run it every day. Below is a full walkthrough of what's included, how the build works, and what it costs.

What This Service Includes

This is a full program build, not a single deliverable. It covers the clinical protocol, the staff who deliver it, the documentation that makes it defensible, and the billing structure that makes it sustainable. Everything is developed specifically for your practice — your space, your staff, your patient population, and your state's requirements.

Program Components

—A documented preparation and delivery protocol, specified by indication
—Staff training to competency, with sign-off records
—AAAHC-aligned policies and procedures written for your practice
—Billing and coding guidance appropriate to your state and payer mix

What You Retain Control Of

You keep full clinical authority. Regenitiva builds and maintains the operational structure around the program — we don't practice medicine, and we don't take that decision away from you or your team.

"We were referring these patients out for years. Now we're the ones who can help them." — Physiatrist, California practice

How We Build It

The build runs in four stages, typically over six to ten weeks depending on your state's requirements and how much clinical infrastructure already exists in your practice.

1
Assessment

We review your space, staff, patient population, and state requirements to scope the program.

2
Protocol & Documentation

We write the clinical protocol, policies, and procedures specific to your practice.

3
Training

Your whole team trains remotely, on your own equipment, until signed off to competency.

4
Launch & Support

You begin treating patients with ongoing auditing and outcome tracking in place.

Why this matters

Programs built without documented training and sign-off records are the ones that don't hold up under a payer audit. We build the record as we build the program, not after something goes wrong.

Compliance & Documentation

Every program includes a complete documentation set, maintained as standards change. Read more about our full approach on the Compliance & Operations page.

Documentation Set

—Clinical protocol and technique standards
—Staff competency and training records
—Internal audit schedule and quality management plan

Timeline & Investment

Every engagement is scoped to the practice, so there isn't a list price. A single physician adding a service line is a different build than a multi-site group standardizing across locations.

Typical Timeline

Most practices are treating patients within six to ten weeks of kickoff. We'll give you a specific figure and a realistic schedule during the assessment — start a conversation to get started.
