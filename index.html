Compliance & Operations

Compliance is what Regenitiva is known for, and it's the reason our programs hold up.

A regenerative medicine service line touches documentation, staff competency, procedure standards, coding, and — for practices treating accident patients — lien handling. Most practice-development firms design the clinical program and hand the operational risk back to the physician. We build the operational structure first and treat the clinical program as something that has to run inside it.

When your program is reviewed — internally, by a payer, or by anyone else — the documentation exists and it's current.

THE PROTOCOL

A protocol, not a kit.

Most practices offering PRP are running a bedside kit and a technique that lives in one person's head. Regenitiva delivers a defined preparation, a documented technique, and a program built to run inside the practice you already have.

MATCHED TO THE INDICATION

Spine, cervical, and joint are not the same problem.

Our preparation and delivery are specified by indication rather than applied uniformly. Each protocol defines the preparation, the approach, and the documentation that goes with it.

BUILT FOR YOUR OFFICE

It runs inside the practice you already have.

Equipment, room setup, chair time, and staff roles are defined before you start. Your team is trained to competency and signed off, so the protocol runs the same whoever is in the room.

A STEP BEFORE THE OPERATING ROOM

Options for the patients who've run out of them.

Many patients arrive having exhausted conservative care but not ready for surgery — and today they get referred out or told to wait. An office-based regenerative program gives you something to offer that group, in your practice, without a hospital referral.

THE PATIENTS YOU CAN HELP

She came in as a surgical candidate. She left pain free and without surgery.

A 45-year-old woman had been told her back would need surgery. She was treated in the office instead, and today she's pain-free — without ever going to the operating room.

Not every patient is a candidate, and not every case ends this way. But this is what a properly built program makes possible: real options for the people sitting in your waiting room right now.

Frequently asked questions.

Does this work clinically?

PRP is used widely in musculoskeletal medicine, and the evidence base continues to develop. We're candid about what the research supports and what it doesn't. Our protocols are built on current literature, and we track your patient outcomes so you can see how your own program performs.

How is this billed?

That depends on your payer mix, your state, and your patient population. For practices treating motor vehicle accident patients, lien management is often central. We build a billing and documentation approach appropriate to your situation and within accepted standards.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is scoped to the practice, so there isn't a list price. A single physician adding a service line is a different build than a multi-site group standardizing across locations. What drives the number is the scope of the program, how much clinical and staff infrastructure already exists, and whether you want ongoing management or a one-time implementation. We put a specific figure and a projected return in front of you during the assessment — before you're asked to commit to anything.

How long until we're treating patients?

Timeline depends on your space, staffing, and state requirements. We'll give you a realistic schedule during the assessment, not an optimistic one.

Do I give up control of my practice?

No. You retain clinical authority and ownership. We manage operations, compliance, and business infrastructure.

What happens to my existing staff and workflows?

We build around your team. Competency training brings existing staff into the program rather than replacing them, and workflow design starts from how your practice already runs.
